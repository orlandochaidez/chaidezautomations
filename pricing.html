How far do the minutes actually go?

A typical call — hello, what they need, name and number, booking the estimate — takes about 3 minutes. So 500 minutes covers roughly 150+ calls a month, and 1,500 minutes covers around 500 calls. Most small shops use a fraction of that. And if your shop ever regularly needs more, we'll flag it and talk through options — you'll never get a surprise bill from us.

Your customers can text you too — unlimited texts

Plenty of customers would rather text than call. With customer texting, they text your business number and the assistant replies right away — day or night, in English or Spanish. It answers the everyday questions (hours, services, where you work), takes their info, and helps book the estimate — just like a call, but in writing. And every conversation shows up in your summary, same as a call. Unlimited texts — no per-message charges. Included in Front Desk Complete; add it to Front Desk for $100/mo.

Quoting is your choice — it can give rough estimates for simple jobs, or never mention price at all. Either way, your technician confirms the final price on site.
